How it works & notes
  • Card names are downloaded once from Scryfall and cached locally for 7 days.
  • Multi-word names are matched longest-first; matching ignores accents and punctuation.
  • Double-faced, split & adventure cards are matched by either face name — so Sea Gate Restoration resolves correctly instead of falling through to Sea Gate. Details are fetched by the front-face name, which Scryfall's collection endpoint accepts (the combined “A // B” name is rejected).
  • If both faces of one card are mentioned, they collapse into a single tile (marked DFC) with the counts summed.
  • Require capitalization compares each word's casing to the official name (so connector words like “the” are exempt) — great for prose, but uncheck it for all-lowercase decklists.
  • Color groups by the relevant face's printed colors; Color Identity groups by guild/shard/wedge (what matters for Commander).
  • Common-word names (Fog, Giant, Island) may still slip through — click any tile to exclude it.
Card names, data, and images provided by the Scryfall API. Card images retain the original artist and copyright. Unofficial tool, not endorsed by Scryfall or Wizards of the Coast.
