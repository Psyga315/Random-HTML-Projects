Gemini Video Shrinker

Shrinks video to roughly what Gemini actually looks at (≈1 fps, low resolution, mono audio), then lets you ask Gemini about it through the OpenRouter API, since OpenRouter's chatroom doesn't accept video. Compression runs locally; files only leave your computer when you press Send.

Drop video files here or click to choose MP4, MOV, MKV, WebM, MPEG-TS (also audio files). AVI/WMV/FLV aren't supported; use the ffmpeg command below.
