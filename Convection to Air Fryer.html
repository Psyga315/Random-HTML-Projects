💡 Tips for Best Results

    ⚠️ Important Safety Information

    These results are estimates only. Always verify food is cooked safely before eating.

    • Verify internal temperature with a food thermometer (USDA minimums): poultry 165 °F / 74 °C, ground meats 160 °F / 71 °C, whole cuts of beef/pork/lamb 145 °F / 63 °C + 3-min rest, fish/seafood 145 °F / 63 °C, reheated leftovers 165 °F / 74 °C. Reference: USDA FSIS Safe Temperature Chart.
    • Appliance variation is significant: air fryer brand, wattage, basket size, fill level and age all affect cook time. Check food early and often, especially the first time you cook a product.
    • At high altitude (above ~3,000 ft / 900 m), cooking times can be longer and temperatures may need small adjustments — monitor carefully.
    • Preheat the air fryer 2–3 minutes before adding food; the converted time assumes a preheated basket.
    • Do not overcrowd the basket. Cook in batches for best results.
    • This tool does not replace manufacturer instructions. Follow all safety guidance for your specific appliance and food product.
